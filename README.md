Tohitapu – Tongan & English Bible (website version)

HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (free, about 5 minutes, easiest on a computer):
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up (free).
3. Drag the whole unzipped folder onto the page.
4. Netlify gives you a link (you can rename it, e.g. tohitapu.netlify.app).

HOW PEOPLE INSTALL IT:
- Android (Chrome): open the link, tap the menu (three dots) and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
- iPhone (Safari): open the link, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
After the first visit the whole Bible is saved on the phone and works with no internet.

Texts: Tongan Revised West Version (public domain) and King James Version (public domain), via eBible.org.
